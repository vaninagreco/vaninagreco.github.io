Launching ESG App: A Top Performer with 42% of Sales

Role
UX UI designer and UX leader
Period
May 2021 – July 2021
Sector
Financial compliance
Team
UX UI designer and 1 product owner

Overview

The company* is a web platform that helps legal and financial professionals manage regulatory documents and stay compliant with the SEC (U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission), offering 15+ applications for specialized searches and reporting tasks.

*Company name is hidden for confidentiality reasons.

With the surge in ESG issues, which are central to public opinion, companies are required to be transparent with stakeholders on issues such as climate risk and workplace diversity.

That's why we saw a need for a new ESG app on our platform to help companies stay ahead of their peers on ESG legal & regulatory matters, evaluate risks, and develop disclosure strategies.

The goal of this research is to accurately validate and evaluate the market’s need for this new application.

Problem statement

What are the real needs of our target users in ESG research, and how can we meet them?

Outcomes

A research-led launch that became the best-selling app in the range, accounting for 42% of sales.

42%of sales: the top performer in the product range

A team effort

For this project, I worked closely with the product owner, an experienced legal researcher.

She handled matters requiring her domain expertise, such as business feasibility and desk research. She also led the user interviews. I analyzed the information, created deliverables such as affinity maps, presentations, and mockups, and validated usability through user testing.

Research process

Desk research

The PO did extensive research, including a competitor benchmark and some proto personas.

Domain expert interview

We interviewed the director of the SEC institute division, who is also a speaker and professor.

User interviews

We performed 5 user interviews, all with advanced users of the platform (2 Research Managers and 3 Senior Analysts).

We asked them about:

  • Their current ESG research needs and process
  • Their preferred tools
  • Their opinions on the ratings, frameworks, and model disclosure available

We analyzed the insights using affinity maps to identify the users' needs and pain points for the new app.

Key insights

Users look for ESG company profiles, disclosure models, and reports to model their own and stay current with their industry and peers.

They also struggle to find the right framework and standard.

Some actual quotes from users:

  • Attorneys are looking at top tech companies and looking at the ratings, and has the rating changed in the past year depending on what they released?

  • Typical question: what does X company do, do they have a policy, is it a press release only or is it a formal policy?

  • Having something the attorneys can model what they are writing themselves is really the ultimate goal.

User testing

I ran an unmoderated test on a clickable prototype in Maze. 5 users completed the test, most of them internal, due to confidentiality concerns.

They had to complete the following task:

“Find examples of companies disclosing user privacy issues”

The main goal was to reach an ESG Report in the Document View (the Document View is where you open an actual document, such as a PDF ESG report, after searching for it).

Test results

  • 4 of the 5 users reached the report, while 1 gave up.
  • Of the 4 successful users, half clicked the Topics Table first (the table at the top of the screen that explores current ESG research topics, such as air quality and data security), while the others used the left panel to apply filters.
  • The average time on screen was 7.7 seconds, which is a great score.
  • All 4 of our survey respondents gave positive answers, ranging from “relatively easy” to “super easy”.

Closing words

This project opened the door to research in a highly regulated and conservative company. Its focus on user research and testing gave it an advantage, making the ESG App one of the bestsellers in the product range. Since its launch in August 2021, it has been a main driver of the portfolio and is, as of this date, the top performer in the range, with 42% of sales.

These results made it an example and opened the door to more user-centered, data-driven projects and activities at the company.
